# 05 — Quatre pratiques autonomes avec critères explicites

**Protocole commun :** Each prompt is new. Before drafting, write **Audience: …**, **Purpose: to …**, then a small two-part plan (P1/P2). Write **exactly two ordered English paragraphs** (several connected sentences per paragraph, ordinarily 2–4 for practice). Reread and mark one change that makes the text more helpful for your readers. No content hints or copied model text. Dictée vocale possible si l’élève relit et révise sa transcription.

## P1 — A craft activity for a new student

**Prompt:** “A new student will join a pretend paper-craft group. Explain how to start the activity and what to do with materials afterwards.” Facts available: coloured paper, safe paper shapes, glue stick, clean-up tray.

**Réponse attendue / critères :** audience = new group member; purpose = to explain; P1 prepares the materials and starting steps; P2 clean-up and return steps; two paragraphs in a sensible order with complete, understandable English sentences. Variation libre de détails plausibles; ne pas exiger que l’élève ait vécu l’activité.

## P2 — Introduce a mini-exhibition to visitors

**Prompt:** “Visitors are coming to a pretend exhibition of invented objects. Write a two-paragraph guide describing what visitors will see and how to explore the exhibits respectfully.” Objects may be entirely imaginary.

**Réponse attendue / critères :** audience = exhibition visitors; purpose = to describe/explain; P1 introduces exhibits (e.g., a story-telling lamp); P2 explains how to tour and behave; enough clear English for an unfamiliar reader. Penalize neither fantasy nor unconventional creative choices if communicated logically.

## P3 — Invite another class to a book swap

**Prompt:** “Write an invitation to another class for a fictional book-swap day. Tell readers what the event is and how they can take part.” Facts: pupils may bring one *imaginary or real* book description, exchange recommendations, and take their own books home. No date or personal contact is needed.

**Réponse attendue / critères :** audience = another class; purpose = to invite; P1 invitation and nature of event; P2 how to participate / welcoming ending; avoid fabricated fixed time/place claims. A persuasive invitation may differ in wording while respecting coherence and correctness.

## P4 — Suggest a pretend play-space improvement

**Prompt:** “Write to a fictional student group proposing a simple improvement to a pretend indoor play area. Explain why it may help and how pupils could take care of it.” Possible improvement (choose your own): puzzle table, board-game corner, soft-seat reading spot.

**Réponse attendue / critères :** audience = student group; purpose = to persuade; P1 suggestion and a reason; P2 practical care/use and request; two paragraphs, relevant detail, comprehensible English. Arguments may be imaginative and do not require evidence about real people.

## Grille rapide pour chaque pratique (non décision finale de maîtrise)

- **D1 Reader & purpose:** a named group and an appropriate *to + verb* intention, reflected in details.
- **D2 Plan & paragraph jobs:** two different, ordered ideas; visible break; details grouped sensibly.
- **D3 English for meaning:** complete sentences, understandable verbs/references, punctuation supporting clarity; local errors permissible if meaning stays clear.
- **D4 Self-direction:** learner prepared the plan, drafted, checked and revised without an adult supplying ideas or sentences.

**Feedback après chaque essai :** indiquer une preuve observable de réussite et une seule priorité de révision. Exemple : “Your first paragraph tells visitors what they will see. In paragraph two, add how they can move through the exhibit.” **Tracer les aides**, distinctes des outils accessibles. P1–P4 sont de l’entraînement, non un remplacement des deux contextes d’évaluation Q009 et Q010.
