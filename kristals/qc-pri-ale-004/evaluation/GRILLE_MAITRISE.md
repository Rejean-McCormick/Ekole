# Mastery rubric — Narrative comprehension (qc-pri-ale-004)

**Objective `obj-1`:** Reconstituer actions, personnages et idées d’un récit en anglais. **Criterion `crit-1`:** Résumer une histoire et citer un détail qui soutient le résumé. **Family:** `qc-pri-ale-004-ev-01`. Observe independently produced meaning, *not* memorized model phrasing.

## Four observable dimensions, each marked 0–3 on every task

| Dimension | 0 — not demonstrated | 1 — developing | 2 — satisfactory, independent | 3 — secure and clear |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1. Characters and roles** | Invents the central character or cannot identify anyone acting. | Names a character but confuses who performs an important action. | Identifies the central character(s) and essential actions correctly. | Identifies central and supporting roles precisely without unnecessary detail. |
| **C2. Plot, key events and order** | Contradicts the text or has no recognizable sequence. | Includes some real events, but major cause/action/outcome is absent or reversed. | Reconstructs the central problem/change, action and outcome in coherent temporal or causal order. | Selects only the most significant linked events and expresses why the outcome follows. |
| **C3. Main-idea summary** | Blank, unrelated or dominated by invented claims. | Broad/vague list or partially inaccurate paraphrase; weak cohesion. | Gives a concise English summary retaining central meaning without ungrounded additions. | Concise, fluent summary integrates central situation and change with accurate causal logic. |
| **C4. Linked text evidence** | No textual detail or fabricated quotation. | Quotes irrelevant words, or details are too vague to support a claim. | Quotes exactly or identifies/paraphrases a specific *relevant* detail from the text and links it to a summary claim. | Chooses a decisive detail and explicitly explains how that wording supports the claim. |

**Scoring note:** The first independent attempt determines mastery evidence. Later revisions inform teaching but do not retroactively turn a prompted attempt into independent performance. Distinct correct summaries and alternate valid quotations are acceptable when the passage supports them. Mispronunciation, handwriting, and minor grammar or spelling errors that do not obscure comprehension do not automatically lower the rubric; invented or reversed plot facts do.

## Two mandatory, distinct demonstrations (not one averaged score)

| Proof | Bank item | New context | Conditions | Minimum decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | `qc-pri-ale-004-q-011` | Roof garden / dry herbs / revised watering schedule | Learner independently reads a new passage and gives an unprompted English summary + one linked detail. | C1, C2, C3 and C4 each **≥2**. |
| M2 | `qc-pri-ale-004-q-012` | Rolling community library / damaged wheel / temporary service | Different new text and day/activity; same independent evidence. | C1, C2, C3 and C4 each **≥2**. |

**Mastery = both rows independently satisfactory**, with four dimensions checked separately. A score of 10/12 that hides a score 1 in evidence is *not* mastery; scoring the same story twice does not create a second context. A separate diagnostic may demonstrate equivalence to prerequisite `qc-pri-ale-003`; oral-only story listening is not proof of autonomous print reading.

## Decision and remediation (dimension-specific)

- **If C1 below 2:** retell with verbs underlined to ask *who* acts, then remove the help for a new story.
- **If C2 below 2:** sequence three event strips, explain what causes the resolution, then retry on a fresh narrative.
- **If C3 below 2:** contrast a list of setting/object details with a coherent problem-response-outcome sentence, then independently summarize a new passage.
- **If C4 below 2:** build a claim/evidence chart; practice selecting and explaining a relevant quotation; then use a different source without the chart.
- **If reading access prevents an independent performance:** provide appropriate accessibility supports, assess or strengthen reading fluency, and document the reading condition; do not falsely declare decoding demonstrated by teacher narration.
- **If M1 passes and M2 does not:** do not award mastery; address M2 weak dimension(s), then use a third previously unseen text for retest, with a human-reviewed fixed key. Do not recycle the already disclosed item as a new cold context.
- **If both pass:** mark mastery only after a qualified human reviews the two recordings/texts and confirms any accommodations. Follow up with 2- and 7-day short retrieval/transfer opportunities.

**Assessment provenance:** source-locked, scripted keys and rubrics are editorial; they require review of possible ambiguity, accessibility in LMS, linguistic correctness and bias prior to learner assessment. The items are `a_revoir`, not calibrated or certified.
