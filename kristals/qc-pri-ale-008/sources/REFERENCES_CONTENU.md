## C. Reasoned pedagogical derivations (not quotations)

- The evidence structure “meaning + morphological analysis + contextual clue” operationalizes the immutable objective and criterion; the 8-point per-pair rubric is a local editorial design decision, not an official benchmark.
- Controlled fixed texts are used instead of unpredictable AI-generated assessment items, because sense depends on context and open answers need human judgment. Initial/intermediate/transfer levels are uncalibrated author labels.
- Explanations such as *thoughtless* = inconsiderate or *restless* = unable to settle are contextual readings, **not** algebraic sums of suffixes; lexical interpretation governs where morphology underdetermines meaning.
