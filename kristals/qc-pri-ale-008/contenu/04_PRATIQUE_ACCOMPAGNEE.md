# 04 — Three guided tasks with fading prompts

Work on **two English sentences** per task and insist on a meaning, a relevant part and a context phrase for **both** words. Remove the hints progressively and record whether the student still needs them. These are teaching items, **not** evidence items in the bank.

## G1 — Fully prompted (first pass)

**1.** “The door was **unlocked**, so the students could open it without a key.”  
**2.** “Tess packed a **colorful** poster covered in bright red, blue and yellow patterns.”

**Hints:** Box `un-` and `-ful`. Underline “without a key” and “bright red, blue and yellow”. Find the roots `lock` and `color`. Say: “I think ... because ...”.

**Reference:** *unlocked* = not locked/openable; `un- + lock + -ed`, clue “without a key”. *colorful* = full of many vivid colors; `color + -ful`, clue “bright red, blue and yellow patterns.”

**Feedback:** If the response for *unlocked* is “no door”, reread “could open it”; the door still exists.

## G2 — Question prompts, no segmentation given

**1.** “The drawing was **unfinished**; its outline was complete, but the artist had not added any details.”  
**2.** “A **playful** puppy chased a ball and bounced around the yard.”

**Hints (only questions):** What happened or was missing in each sentence? What base do you recognize? Where is the added piece? Which exact phrase confirms the guess?

**Reference:** *unfinished* = not completed; `un- + finish + -ed`, clue “had not added any details”. *playful* = full of play or eager to play; `play + -ful`, clue “chased a ball and bounced”.

**Feedback:** “*Unfinished* is not *not started*; the outline is complete. Check the whole sentence.”

## G3 — Minimal support; student leads

**1.** “Luca **rechecked** his answer because the first calculation seemed wrong.”  
**2.** “The **treeless** hill had only grass and rocks, with no trunks or branches in sight.”

**Prompt only:** Infer and explain both target words; include a word part and contextual evidence. A neutral “Show me where you found that clue” is permitted after the first attempt. No other hint until feedback.

**Reference:** *rechecked* = checked again; `re- + check + -ed`, clue “first calculation seemed wrong”. *treeless* = without trees; `tree + -less`, clue “only grass and rocks”/“no trunks or branches”.

**Feedback:** If *treeless* is explained as “fewer trees,” distinguish “no trees” from “fewer than before”; `-less` and the explicit description support absence here.

## Fade-out decision

- If G1 fails, compare prefix and suffix with a card and retry a NEW sentence.
- If G2 fails, prompt only the missing kind of evidence (part **or** context) then retry.
- If G3 meets all six checks (M, P, C for both words), proceed to autonomous A1–A4 without hints.
- Accessible alternatives: spoken explanation captured by a scribe; original English text still visible and word segmentation performed by the learner, not the adult.
