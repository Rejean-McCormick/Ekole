# 02 — Brief diagnostic, before teaching

No prior Kristal is required. Give the two tasks before the worked examples; do not turn the diagnostic into an admission barrier. **Do not present the keys until after responding.** Each sentence is an original text.

## D1 — Oral explanation plus written annotation (about 4 minutes)

**S1:** “The directions were unclear, so Farah asked the teacher to explain them in a simpler way.” Target: **unclear**.  
**S2:** “After the rain stopped, the children replayed the game because the first round had been interrupted.” Target: **replayed**.

**Prompt:** For each word, explain its probable meaning. Name a meaningful word part. Underline or quote a clue in the sentence. Let a learner explain aloud then write or dictate a short statement; record whether oral reasoning exceeds written notation.

**Key:** *unclear* = difficult to understand/not clear; `un- + clear`; clue “asked ... to explain ... in a simpler way.” *replayed* = played again; `re- + play + -ed`; clue “first round had been interrupted.” **Do not require** a learner to label `-ed` to receive evidence for `re-`.

## D2 — Independent written pair (about 4 minutes)

**S1:** “The picnic table was **spotless** after Mia wiped away every crumb and stain.”  
**S2:** “The sign was **misprinted**, so the date on it was wrong and the staff made a new copy.”

**Prompt:** Give the meaning of each bold word **in this sentence** and justify it with morphology and an exact context phrase. No hints during this attempt.

**Key:** *spotless* = without spots/marks; `spot + -less`; clue “wiped away every crumb and stain.” *misprinted* = printed incorrectly; `mis- + print + -ed`; clue “date ... was wrong” and “new copy.”

## Observation and decision

For each target use three checks: **M** meaning accurate; **P** meaningful part named correctly; **C** a quoted context clue supports the meaning. Four targets × 3 = 12 observations; **this is not a mastery grade**.

- Correct meaning + P + C for all 4 without prompts: short route to independent practice, then two new evidence contexts.
- Meaning right but no C: teach quote-the-clue using `03_ENSEIGNEMENT_GUIDE.md`.
- P wrong but C right: teach prefix/suffix/root contrast; never claim a prerequisite is missing.
- Both incorrect: oral think-aloud and a single shorter sentence with annotated clue; retry later using different words.

**Access:** Read instructions aloud if needed; display original English words and sentences unchanged. Allow typing, enlarged print or dictation of reasoning, noting any prompts supplied.
