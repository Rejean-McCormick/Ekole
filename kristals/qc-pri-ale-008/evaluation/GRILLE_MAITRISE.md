# Mastery rubric — `qc-pri-ale-008`

**Objective `obj-1`:** Déduire le sens de mots anglais en utilisant contexte et morphologie.  
**Criterion `crit-1` (immutable):** Expliquer le sens de mots nouveaux dans deux phrases.  
**Family:** `qc-pri-ale-008-ev-01` — argued response with a controlled fixed text.

## Observables per target in an unfamiliar English sentence

| Dimension | 0 — Not yet | 1 — Developing | 2 — Secure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meaning in context** | Incorrect, opposite or missing | Broadly plausible but vague/partly wrong | Accurate specific sense fitting this sentence |
| **Morphological reasoning** | No genuine part, or false split | Names a relevant actual part but cannot explain its function | Correct root/base and affix, with how the affix contributes |
| **Contextual justification** | No actual text clue / invented clue | Actual clue quoted but relevance unclear | Precise actual clue and explicit connection to sense |
| **Independence** | Leading questions or answer supplied | Neutral support beyond allowed accommodation required | Independently supplies reasoning without hints, dictionary, feedback or segmentation |

**Operational scoring for a single item (2 targets):** each target receives meaning 0–2, morpheme valid 0/1, context clue 0/1; 4 points per target / 8 per item. In the qualitative level table above, '2 — Secure' for morphology or contextual justification indicates a strong explanation but still counts for one point in the item scoring. Feedback can be descriptive rather than numeric; handwriting accuracy and accent do not enter the rubric.

## Two independent manifestations in different contexts

- **Proof A — `qc-pri-ale-008-q-011`:** Community garden, target words *replant* and *unprotected*. The learner explains two new words in two fresh English sentences with word-part analysis and quoted clues.
- **Proof B — `qc-pri-ale-008-q-012`:** Weather science report, target words *unpredictable* and *refreeze*. Same cognitive demands but materially different context, sentence structure and combinations of affixes.
- Present them separately without sharing their answer keys. Do not precede either with a demonstration of its target words. Allow neutral physical/accessibility accommodations; do not provide meaning clues.

## Decision rule (not a single grade)

**Mastery demonstrated:** in EACH of A and B, both target meanings are accurate in that sentence (2/2 for each), the learner identifies real word parts and their relevant contribution (1/1 each), and connects an actual textual clue to the explanation (1/1 each); reasoned autonomous delivery observed in both sessions. Thus both performances are complete (8/8 each), but the decision also independently checks contextual diversity and absence of help. Alternative fluent explanations are accepted if analytically equivalent.

**Mastery developing:** any material element missing or a task finished with concept hints. Keep specific feedback (meaning, word part, context), reteach that link and collect a genuinely new **pre-reviewed** two-sentence pair in another context; do not merely replay and score a memorized answer. Failure on one task does not erase the other valid evidence, but does prevent a completed mastery decision pending an independent retest.

**Needs focused support:** repeated false segmentation → affix/root sorting; unsupported guesses → highlight context and reason; context meaning wrong despite morphological clue → compare candidate meanings against sentence facts. Track success on new words after repair.

**Not evidence of mastery:** one correct score, selecting a multiple-choice option, an English-language accent, remembering the teacher's exact sentence, age/grade label, or a generated response scored solely by a model.

**Post-learning retention:** run the new retrieval pair from `contenu/06_REMEDIATION_ET_TRANSFERT.md` after 2–7 days; treat as maintenance information, not as a replacement for two independent proof contexts.
