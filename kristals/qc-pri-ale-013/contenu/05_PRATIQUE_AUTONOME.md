# 05 — Quatre pratiques autonomes

**Règle commune :** une lecture par deux voix, puis une seconde lecture intégrale du même script; aucun indice sur l'information centrale. L'élève répond en anglais à la consigne “Summarize the main idea in one or two sentences, add two accurate details, and ask one relevant clarification question about something the speakers did not tell you.” Chaque dialogue et son corrigé servent au facilitateur; ne pas projeter le script avant les réponses. Acceptation de formulations différentes mais factuellement identiques. Ces essais sont formatifs, distincts des deux preuves finales q-011/q-012.

## P1 — Plants and observations (q-006)

**Dialogue 06 — Plant observations** (script enseignant, non montré avant la réponse)

```text
Nina: Our plant team has finished setting up the seedlings. What happens next?
Owen: Starting tomorrow, we will check the leaves every day and note their colour.
Nina: Should we water the plants at each check?
Owen: Not automatically. First we observe. The teacher has not said where we will store the notebooks.
```

**Tâche élève (en anglais) :** Listen to the conversation twice. Give a one- or two-sentence summary of its main idea, include two accurate supporting details, and ask one useful clarification question about information the speakers have not provided.

**Corrigé repère :** The team will begin daily observations of the seedlings and record leaf colour. Détails : They begin tomorrow. / They should observe before deciding whether to water. Question de clarification possible : Where will we store our observation notebooks?

**Indice oral :** Starting tomorrow marks the time; not automatically corrects an assumption.

## P2 — Picnic plan (q-007)

**Dialogue 07 — Picnic location** (script enseignant, non montré avant la réponse)

```text
Nora: Should we eat near the river or in the park?
Eli: The path near the river is closed. Instead, we can use the shaded picnic area in the park.
Nora: That makes sense. Do we know when to leave school?
Eli: Not yet. We will get the departure time tomorrow.
```

**Tâche élève (en anglais) :** Listen to the conversation twice. Give a one- or two-sentence summary of its main idea, include two accurate supporting details, and ask one useful clarification question about information the speakers have not provided.

**Corrigé repère :** The group will use the shaded park picnic area rather than the closed riverside path. Détails : The riverside path is closed. / The park has a shaded picnic area. Question de clarification possible : What time will we leave school?

**Indice oral :** Instead replaces an unavailable choice with a possible one.

## P3 — Newsletter deadline (q-008)

**Dialogue 08 — School newsletter** (script enseignant, non montré avant la réponse)

```text
Drew: Can I still send my article for the school newsletter on Wednesday?
Kai: Actually, the deadline is now Monday because the editor needs more time.
Drew: Then I will finish it this weekend. Do I email it?
Kai: Yes, but I need to check which address we should use.
```

**Tâche élève (en anglais) :** Listen to the conversation twice. Give a one- or two-sentence summary of its main idea, include two accurate supporting details, and ask one useful clarification question about information the speakers have not provided.

**Corrigé repère :** The school newsletter deadline moved earlier, from Wednesday to Monday. Détails : The editor needs more time. / Articles will be emailed. Question de clarification possible : Which email address should I use?

**Indice oral :** Actually announces a correction; then indicates the speaker’s plan.

## P4 — Drama rehearsal (q-009)

**Dialogue 09 — Drama rehearsal** (script enseignant, non montré avant la réponse)

```text
Leah: Are we practising our scene in the courtyard?
Amir: No, the wind is too strong, so we will use the main stage at school.
Leah: Do we arrive at three?
Amir: Please come at ten past three. Nobody has confirmed whether costumes are needed.
```

**Tâche élève (en anglais) :** Listen to the conversation twice. Give a one- or two-sentence summary of its main idea, include two accurate supporting details, and ask one useful clarification question about information the speakers have not provided.

**Corrigé repère :** Drama rehearsal moved from the courtyard to the main stage because of wind. Détails : Students should arrive at 3:10. / Strong wind prompted the change. Question de clarification possible : Do we need to bring costumes?

**Indice oral :** So connects the wind to the location change; no rejects the old location.

## Grille commune de correction formative

- **Main idea (0–2)** : 2 = changement/objectif central exact; 1 = sujet reconnu sans décision; 0 = contresens.
- **Two details (0–2)** : 1 point pour chaque détail distinct explicitement dit, jusqu'à 2; pas de point pour invention.
- **Clarification (0–2)** : 2 = question pertinente sur un manque réel, 1 = utile mais vague, 0 = déjà répondu/hors sujet.
- **Fidelity (0–2)** : 2 = sépare informations entendues et déductions; 1 = légère approximation sans inversion; 0 = contradiction importante ou plusieurs inventions.
- **Feedback après chaque essai** : nommer un fait central correct et une seule révision prioritaire; faire vérifier la correction sur une réécoute ou un nouveau dialogue, pas juste recopier la réponse.

**Évaluation non sommative :** ne pas considérer le total de cette pratique comme une preuve suffisante de maîtrise. Ces activités ciblent la préparation de deux situations inédites indépendantes.
