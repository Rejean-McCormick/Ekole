# 04 — Trois exercices guidés, indices dégressifs

**Mode :** dialogues lus à deux voix, deux écoutes, transcription pour le facilitateur uniquement. Les trois items réutilisent les scénarios contrôlés q-003, q-004, q-005, avec consignes en anglais et corrigés documentés.

## G1 — Indices explicites (q-003, livre d'astronomie)

**Dialogue 03 — Missing library book** (script enseignant, non montré avant la réponse)

```text
Priya: I cannot find the astronomy book on the shelf.
Liam: The librarian moved those books to a blue cart this morning.
Priya: Oh, so I should look on the blue cart?
Liam: Exactly. And remember, your library book is due on Friday.
```

**Tâche élève (en anglais) :** Listen to the conversation twice. Give a one- or two-sentence summary of its main idea, include two accurate supporting details, and ask one useful clarification question about information the speakers have not provided.

**Corrigé repère :** Priya learns where to look for the astronomy book that has been moved. Détails : The astronomy books were moved to a blue cart. / Priya has a library book due on Friday. Question de clarification possible : Where is the blue cart?

**Indice oral :** So restates the action; exactly confirms the listener understood.

**Indices fournis avant réponse :** 1) “Listen for *moved*.” 2) Remplir : “The book is now ___.” 3) Demander : “What useful location has NOT been specified?”

**Rétroaction attendue :** « Blue cart » est un emplacement général connu; « Where is the blue cart? » est une clarification encore utile. Ne pas accorder de point pour « Is the book on the shelf? » si le déplacement est explicitement annoncé.

## G2 — Indice minimal (q-004, music club)

**Dialogue 04 — Music club** (script enseignant, non montré avant la réponse)

```text
Jules: Does music club still begin at four today?
Robin: No. Because the hall is in use, we will start at four thirty in Room Eight.
Jules: Four thirty in Room Eight. Thanks for telling me.
Robin: You are welcome. I do not know how long rehearsal will last.
```

**Tâche élève (en anglais) :** Listen to the conversation twice. Give a one- or two-sentence summary of its main idea, include two accurate supporting details, and ask one useful clarification question about information the speakers have not provided.

**Corrigé repère :** Music club starts at 4:30 in Room Eight instead of 4:00. Détails : The hall is in use. / The updated start time is 4:30. Question de clarification possible : How long will rehearsal last?

**Indice oral :** No corrects the old time; because introduces the reason.

**Indice unique :** “Notice whether the first time is confirmed or corrected.” L'élève choisit seul le nouvel horaire, les deux détails et une question. **Rétroaction :** distinguer 4:00 ancien et 4:30 nouveau; la durée est inconnue.

## G3 — Sans indice préalable (q-005, games day)

**Dialogue 05 — Indoor games day** (script enseignant, non montré avant la réponse)

```text
Asha: Will games day be outside tomorrow?
Ben: The forecast says rain, so we are moving all activities indoors.
Asha: Where should we meet?
Ben: At the gym entrance at nine in the morning. We have not chosen which games to play yet.
```

**Tâche élève (en anglais) :** Listen to the conversation twice. Give a one- or two-sentence summary of its main idea, include two accurate supporting details, and ask one useful clarification question about information the speakers have not provided.

**Corrigé repère :** Games day will take place indoors because of the rain forecast. Détails : Students meet at the gym entrance. / The meeting time is 9:00 a.m. Question de clarification possible : Which games will we play inside?

**Indice oral :** So introduces the decision based on the rain forecast.

**Aucun indice avant réponse.** Après production, question métacognitive : “Which spoken word helped you understand the change?” Réponse possible : *so* relie la météo au changement. **Correction :** accepter une reformulation fidèle; faire reprendre uniquement la dimension erronée, puis réessayer un autre scénario.

## Retrait du guidage : feuille simple

| Étape | Avant l'écoute | Après l'écoute |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Indices verbaux + cases structurées | Comparaison commentée au modèle |
| G2 | Une question d'attention | Justification par un élément dit |
| G3 | Seule consigne | Grille de feedback, sans réponse soufflée |

**Suivi :** indiquer séparément main idea / détail / clarification / fait non inventé; ne pas déclarer la maîtrise à partir de ces entraînements.
