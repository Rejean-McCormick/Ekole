## B. Editorial derivations (not source quotations)

- Restricting the corpus to carefully selected CVC words plus `sh`/`ch` is a controlled **instructional scope**, not a claim that English has only these patterns.
- Recognizing `sh` and `ch` as one phoneme in the chosen target words allows deterministic phonemic answers, while dialect/accent and speech access still require human evaluation.
- Maintaining two unseen 4-word sets, requiring at least 3/4 correct decoding **and** clues plus each target digraph, and distinguishing M1–M5 are internal **rubric choices**, not externally normed psychometric criteria.
- Keeping word stimuli and answer keys unchanged for all 10 bank entries prevents free-generation conflicts; no parametric model, formula, solver or randomness is relevant to reading individual fixed words.

## D. Rights, privacy, limitations

- No external pictures, logos, music, recorded voices, copyrighted worksheets or lengthy passages are embedded. External URLs are citations, not redistribution of third-party content. Brief English common words and original prose are the teaching material.
- No minor's voice or personal reading data is collected by these files; optional local paper notes follow an educator’s privacy policy. Any future recording requires consent and governance.
- A created word list is not an experimental proof of diagnostic reliability or disability-equivalent access. Before a consequential judgment, obtain human phonological, English-dialect, accessibility and psychometric review, plus supervised try-outs.
- No PFEQ compliance or official approval asserted. Source citations or content ownership do not settle all legal questions about downstream project licensing.
