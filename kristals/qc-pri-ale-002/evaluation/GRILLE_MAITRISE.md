# Mastery rubric | `qc-pri-ale-002`

`obj-1`: Appliquer des correspondances lettres-sons anglaises au décodage.  
`crit-1`: Lire de nouveaux mots réguliers et indiquer l’indice utilisé.  
**Family:** `qc-pri-ale-002-ev-01` / `réponse_argumentée`.

## Evidence to collect

**Proof A (`qc-pri-ale-002-q-009`)** — independently decode four **never-practised** printed journal labels and give a printed grapheme–sound clue for each: `hen`, `pin`, `rug`, `shed`.  
**Proof B (`qc-pri-ale-002-q-010`)** — independently decode four **different** printed workshop labels with a grapheme–sound clue: `web`, `fin`, `mug`, `chip`.

For each observed word, record: exact printed word; learner's first spoken form or controlled sound evidence; link to letters indicated; whether it was self-corrected; the existence of an adult cue, accommodation and possible dialect/articulation note. **No averaging away an essential criterion between A and B.**

## Anchored levels for each proof

| Dimension | 0: not demonstrated | 1: developing / incomplete | 2: sufficient, observable |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M1 — Correct decoding** | 0–1 of 4 words read from print | 2 of 4 | ≥3 of 4 recognized in an acceptable English realization (access adaptations documented) |
| **M2 — Spelling–sound evidence** | 0–1 of 4 relevant clues | 2 of 4 | ≥3 of 4 accurately printed grapheme→spoken sound clues |
| **M3 — Target digraph** | `sh`/`ch` not read correctly or clue absent | Grapheme recognized but sound wrong, or sound right with no evidence clue | A: `shed` includes /ʃ/ **and** correct `sh` clue; B: `chip` includes /tʃ/ **and** correct `ch` clue |
| **M4 — Blending process** | Letter names or picture guesses alone | Some phoneme joining visible, but adult model substantially needed | Learner independently maps left to right and joins sounds on new items; pauses and self-correction allowed |
| **M5 — Autonomy / fair access** | Word/model supplied, automatic reader substitutes for pupil, or no usable access | Directive code cue given during a proof, or equivalence is uncertain | Four words attempted without adult code prompts; only non-directive access supports agreed in advance |

The numbers for M1/M2 are **counts**, not percentages. Sound realization is judged by reasonable English dialect acceptability rather than imitation of an examiner's exact accent. IPA is reference notation only.

## Decision rule

- **Documented local mastery:** on **each** proof separately, `M1=2`, `M2=2`, `M3=2`, `M4=2` and `M5=2`; A and B are contextually independent and do not use teaching exemplars. This demonstrates new regular-word decoding and grounded clues on two occasions/contexts.
- **Not yet demonstrated:** a target threshold fails in A or B; retrain **that** sound/graphème or sound blending with **other** words and administer a reviewed third independent set later. Never average A=high/B=low into “mastery.”
- **Inconclusive / access not established:** hearing, articulation or response technology prevents valid observation, or an adult inadvertently gives a decoding answer. Arrange a fair equivalent condition with qualified human input and repeat the assessment; do not label a learner incapable.
- **No automatic marking by AI.** The correction is locked, but human judgment evaluates pronunciation differences, clue validity and accessible evidence.

**Examples of decisions:** A decodes 4/4 but gives only 2 correct clues → M2=1 → not mastered even if B is perfect. A and B both read 3/4 with ≥3/4 clues, both target digraphs correctly read and justified, and independent blending shown → passes local criterion; teacher still verifies speech-access and fairness.
