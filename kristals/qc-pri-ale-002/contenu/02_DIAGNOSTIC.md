# 02 — Diagnostic of oral blending and printed code

**Goal:** establish an equivalent entry skill for the strict prerequisite `qc-pri-ale-001`, then decide if direct phonics instruction is needed. This assessment is **not a diagnosis of a disability** and is not a mastery score. Use small clear cards; avoid pictures supplying a word. Teacher models neither tested word.

## Task D1 — Oral prerequisite (no letters shown)

**Say slowly:** “Listen: /m/ … /æ/ … /p/. What word do those sounds make?” Pause, then “/s/ … /ʌ/ … /n/?”  
**Answers:** *map*, *sun*. Present phonemes, not “muh–a–puh”; pronounced short vowels need a clear reference audio or competent teacher voice. **Evidence of equivalent prerequisite:** blending both sound sequences or correcting one independently after requesting repetition of the stimulus (not receiving the whole word). **Record:** sequence heard; learner's word; any hint; speech access accommodations.

**If D1 fails:** practise oral sound joining in a separate short session first; do **not** insist on completing another Kristal's documents. The prerequisite may be satisfied by later equivalent observation.

## Task D2 — Printed decoding

**Show** `sit` and `bed` in that order, without a picture. **Say:** “Read each word. Point to the letter that helped you say the vowel sound.”

**Answer / criterion:** *sit* /sɪt/ and `i` /ɪ/; *bed* /bɛd/ and `e` /ɛ/. Accept recognizable regional vowels and documented articulation accommodations; an isolated vowel name /aɪ/ or /iː/ used instead of the short vowel should be flagged as a correspondence issue if it alters the word. **Evidence:** actual decoded pronunciation and an accurate printed clue per word.

## Decision table

| D1 oral join | D2 printed code | Instructional response |
|---|---|---|
| Demonstrated | 2/2 | Skip unnecessary review; move to `sh`/`ch`, then fresh independent decoding. |
| Demonstrated | 0–1/2 | Model sound–grapheme mapping and left-to-right blending. |
| Not demonstrated yet | Any | Teach oral sound blending briefly and reassess equivalent prerequisite. |
| Inconclusive because of speech/hearing/access | Any | Arrange equivalent sound-evidence modality with a qualified human; do not infer inability from access. |

**Feedback:** “You found `i` and /ɪ/; now join the first, middle and last sound.” Or “Your sound joining is clear; now let the written graphemes lead your reading.” No one is required to disclose disability or home accent.
