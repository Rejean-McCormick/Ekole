# 05 — Four independent decoding practices (with exact correction)

**Shared directions (English):** “Look at the word. Say the sounds and read the word. Point to the letters that gave you a sound and tell me which sound.” No adult verbal model, no matching picture, no automatic text-to-speech reader. All cards can be enlarged or displayed one at a time. These practices are separate from final proofs 009/010.

## I1 — Notebook cover: `sat`

**Stimulus:** `sat` (print only). **Expected:** /sæt/, **sat**; valid clue `a` spells short /æ/, plus `s` /s/ and `t` /t/. **Successful evidence:** complete word recognized in pupil's spoken realization and at least one valid grapheme–phoneme relation. **Feedback:** If vowel is long /eɪ/, “Check this short `a` in our CVC word; say /æ/ and blend again.”

## I2 — Word strip: `men`

**Stimulus:** `men`. **Expected:** /mɛn/, **men**; valid clue `e` /ɛ/. **Typical slip:** guessing *man* because the first/last letters fit; **feedback:** “The middle letter is `e`, not `a`. What short vowel sound does it spell here?”

## I3 — Classroom message card: `cash`

**Stimulus:** `cash`. **Expected:** /kæʃ/, **cash**; `c | a | sh` = /k/ /æ/ /ʃ/, especially `sh` /ʃ/. **Typical slip:** treat `s` and `h` as independent; **feedback:** “The last two letters make one sound. Mark `sh` together, then blend.”

## I4 — Word explorer: `chin`

**Stimulus:** `chin`. **Expected:** /tʃɪn/, **chin**; `ch` → /tʃ/ and `i` → /ɪ/. **Typical slip:** /k/+/h/ for `ch`; **feedback:** “Here `ch` is one grapheme for /tʃ/. Say it once and join the rest.”

## Required independent evidence

For each I1–I4, the pupil identifies an audible word and at least one **relevant printed clue**, not a picture or memory of the adult's voice. Self-corrections are accepted if initiated without instructions. A different English accent or a documented speech-production difficulty does not automatically equal incorrect decoding; an accessible alternate sound-producing modality requires prior human review. **These cards are practice, not mastery tests.**
