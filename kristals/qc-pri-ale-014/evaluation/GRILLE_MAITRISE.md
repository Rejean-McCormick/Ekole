# Grille de maîtrise observable — `qc-pri-ale-014`

**Immutable objective:** `obj-1` — Participer à un échange oral puis présenter une idée argumentée en anglais.  
**Immutable evidence:** `crit-1` — Soutenir un échange et exposer une idée avec un exemple.  
**Family:** `qc-pri-ale-014-ev-01`; native type `réponse_argumentée` evidenced through live oral interaction plus oral presentation.

## Required assessment moments

- **Context A:** `qc-pri-ale-014-q-009`, **Community Reading Day**, fictional event. Partner gives two fixed lines; learner answers and asks a related question, then gives a distinct mini-presentation.
- **Context B:** `qc-pri-ale-014-q-010`, **School Garden Day**, different proposal and example, same competency. The two contexts cannot be collapsed into one speech.
- In **each**, note **four actual alternating turns** followed by a **separate** idea-reason-example exposition; teacher does not choose content or cue wording. Prepared access accommodations remain possible without artificially inferring missing oral evidence.

## Analytic scale (0 absent / 1 developing / 2 sufficient)

| Dimension | 0 — not demonstrated | 1 — partial / inconsistent | 2 — independently observed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M1 Listening and relevance** | Replies do not address either partner question or no observable listening | Only one prompt is answered appropriately; another is missed | Both given partner prompts are understood and answered in a contextually relevant way; repair requests allowed |
| **M2 Reciprocal turn-taking** | No real partner exchange, or learner gives an uninterrupted monologue | Two-way talk exists but learner omits a pertinent question or ignores the reply | Four alternating turns with a relevant student question and clear space for partner response |
| **M3 Idea and reason** | Presentation absent or claim not identifiable | Idea stated, but reason only repeats it or is loosely connected | Clear proposal with a non-circular explanatory reason related to the fictional scenario |
| **M4 Concrete example** | No illustrative example | Generic assertion or illustration not linked to the reason | One plausible specific illustration explaining how/where/who benefits from the proposal |
| **M5 Intelligibility and autonomy** | Core message cannot be understood even with permissible access; content supplied by evaluator | Meaning partly intelligible, or completion needs directive hints during assessment | Message intelligible to listener without native-accent demand; student produces both interaction and presentation without directive prompts; agreed supports documented |

**Do not grade:** native-accent approximation, preferred stylistic register, charisma, speed, personal worldview, disclosure, or ability to perform on camera. Minor grammar variation is acceptable when intended meaning remains clear. If a partner seeks clarification appropriately, that request can be evidence of interaction, not automatically a loss.

## Independent mastery decision — no single weighted grade

**Documented mastery** requires **M1=M2=M3=M4=M5=2 in both contexts**, with genuinely separate responses and ideas in A and B. This conservative editorial threshold requires teacher calibration before summative use. A context cannot compensate for a missing dimension in the other; do not average M scores.

**Still developing:** at least one M score below 2; note the specific evidence and provide targeted practice followed by a fresh independent situation of equivalent difficulty. **Inconclusive:** a hearing, speech, access, safety or partner-procedure obstacle prevents a fair observation; adjust conditions and obtain human equivalence assessment where necessary rather than declaring deficiency.

**Illustration of decision:** A has M1–M5 all 2; B has M1=2, M2=1, M3=2, M4=2, M5=2 → not yet mastered because reciprocal speaking is incomplete in B, regardless of a high total. Target M2 practice and retest in a controlled distinct context.

## Remediation routing

- M1 → short new listening prompts and contingent replies; distinguish difficulty hearing from language comprehension.
- M2 → practise A/B cards and related-question generation, remove supports as proficiency grows.
- M3 → compare explanatory reasons to circular phrases, then justify a new option.
- M4 → supply a concrete imaginary who–what–where scene in a new presentation.
- M5 → address environmental access, timing and meaningful comprehensibility; for modality substitutions that alter the oral target, obtain a documented professional equivalence ruling.
