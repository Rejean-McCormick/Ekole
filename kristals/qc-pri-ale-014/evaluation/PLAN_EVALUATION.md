## Fixed administration / fair variation

- **All `mode=fixe`**. `seed=null`, `parametres=null`. The partner's words and the offered alternatives for both mastery tasks are fixed; there is no generative AI scoring, random item substitution or hidden change of complexity.
- **Responses are open but controlled by a criterion-referenced rubric.** A learner may choose either option, state a reason and illustrate it with a coherent fictional example; the evaluator records the actual English turn-taking and meaning, not exact word matching. The worked examples in JSON are evaluator models, not scripts for learners.
- **Allowed accommodations before testing:** quiet space, accessible positioning and visual contact, appropriate extra planning time, communication aids routinely used by the learner, nonleading blank organizer, a partner familiar with the procedure. If hearing/speech adaptations fundamentally change the spoken/listening proof, qualified human judgment must decide equivalence rather than granting automatic credit.
- **Disallowed assistance during mastery:** supplying the learner's English wording, suggesting a reason or example, changing the partner question when the learner struggles, modeling the exchange, scoring based on native-speaker accent, asking for private experiences.

## Mastery observations and decision

1. Before each proof, explain the scenario and optionally allow brief preparation. The partner reads only the two fixed lines in the correct order, waits for the student's own relevant question before the second line and does not supply feedback during assessment.
2. Observe **four real alternating speaker turns**: partner–learner–partner–learner. A scripted or spoken paragraph without a responsive exchange does not qualify.
3. Student then gives a distinguishable mini-presentation with **idea, relevant reason, specific example**. One argument may be polished, but no precise wording or accent standard is required.
4. For each context separately, assign M1–M5 according to `GRILLE_MAITRISE.md` and note actual support, clarity and content. Two satisfactory, independent contexts are required; a good grade in A cannot compensate for a missing interaction in B.
5. If one dimension is missing, provide targeted feedback **after** the attempt; remediate then reassess in another controlled situation. Do not change a bank item's scenario in place to manufacture success.

## Equity / data minimization

Assess publicly stated *fictional preferences* only; no sensitive identity, personal history, confidence rating, mimicry or accent ranking. Recording optional with informed institutional consent; evaluator notes alone suffice. Cases deliberately avoid time-dependent factual claims. The rubric is locally authored, non-calibrated and marked `a_revoir`: teacher review, linguistic review and supervised piloting are required before any summative use.
